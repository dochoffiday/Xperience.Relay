# Forms and contacts

Two commands for online marketing data: reading the submissions of a form, and deleting contacts in bulk.

<!-- relay:begin commands -->
| Command | Access | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [`get-form-submissions`](get-form-submissions.md) | Read | Reads the submissions of a Kentico form (BizForm). |
| [`delete-contacts`](delete-contacts.md) | Admin | Bulk-deletes the contacts that match a SQL condition, through Kentico's contact deletion service. |
<!-- relay:end commands -->
